# Responsible Use of Generative AI

Generative AI is neither endorsed nor prohibited. Responsible use can improve
productivity; existing contribution standards apply without special exemptions
or restrictions. This policy may evolve through normal governance processes.

Contributors remain fully accountable for quality, correctness, maintainability,
legal compliance, and suitability. They must understand, review, test, and, where
needed, modify AI output before submitting it. Disclosure of AI assistance is
encouraged, not required.

This policy takes no position on unresolved questions of AI output's copyright,
authorship, or derivation from copyrighted material. Contributors must assess
provenance and licensing, follow existing copyright, licensing, and software
freedom policies, and avoid content whose legal status they cannot reasonably
justify.

Do not disclose confidential, private, security-sensitive, or other non-public
information, including credentials and cryptographic keys, to third-party AI
services without explicit authorization consistent with applicable security and
privacy requirements.

Broad-impact automation, including mass issue filing, patch submissions, or code
changes, requires prior discussion and consensus through appropriate channels
and accountable human oversight.
